    Things to look at

    Based on the latest week's reports for the stores in this view. A store is listed when it is below target 3 days running, more than 10% down on last year, has waste above 2% of sales, has customers more than 10% down on last week, or is missing the latest day's report. The Store Manager and the ROM add a comment to each alert; Head Office ticks it once read. Choose “All weeks” above to see every week of 2026.

    Missing weeks

    Every week from Week 1 of 2026 to last week should have a full report (Monday to Sunday) for each store in this view. The full week arrives in the sales email on the Monday after it, so that's the email to look for.

    Targets by category group

    Each group's own monthly sales target for each store (including VAT). The hub spreads each month's target evenly over its days.

    Category groups

    Each group is a set of department (D…) or category (C…) codes from the daily report. Give people access to a group in Manage people → Sales Tracker access: they then see only that group's figures. After adding a group or changing its codes, press Rebuild group figures so past weeks are included.

    Upload the daily reports

    Drop in the report files that arrive by email each morning (report-51.xlsx, report-52.xlsx …), as many stores at once as you like. Each file is matched to its store by its report number, and the store name inside it is checked. Uploading the same week again replaces it with the newer figures.
