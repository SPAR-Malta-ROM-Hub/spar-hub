Things to watch

Shop by shop

Tap a shop to see its trends. "vs LY" compares only days where the shop also traded last year.

Month-end outlook

Projected = sales so far this month + the remaining daily targets × each shop's achievement over the last 8 weeks. "Needed per day" is what each shop must average from tomorrow to hit the month target. Year to date is sales as % of target.

Compare two periods

Choose what to compare and a date inside the period. The comparison period is the one just before it. Choose "Any dates" to set both periods yourself.

Upload daily report

Pick the report file for one or more shops. Sales, customers and waste fill in for every day in the file, and the department figures below update. Uploading the same week again replaces it with the newer figures.

Alerts

How alerts are worked out

Checked every time figures change: missing entries in the last 7 days · 3+ days in a row below target · week-to-date under 95% · waste over target or rising · basket spend falling vs the previous 8 weeks · fewer customers than last year · a day unusually high or low for its weekday · labour productivity dropping · month-end projection off target.

Month summary

Month target = each weekday's target × how many of that weekday fall in the month. Compare against last month and against what the shop actually sold in the same month last year.
