Daily brief

Ready-to-send text built from the latest gap checks. Copy it into WhatsApp or email for the Store Managers and the supplier reps. Change the number of days to make the lists shorter or longer.

Gaps without stock

Every line on the latest gap check with no stock on the system. “Out for” counts the days since it first appeared on a check without dropping off.

Gaps with stock

The system shows stock but the shelf was empty at the check. The store looks for it and records what it found; Head Office or the ROM records the cause and closes it. “Times listed” shows items that keep coming back.

The daily routine

  1. 1pm gap check: anything listed here has stock on the system but an empty shelf.
  2. The store looks in the back store and on other shelves, refills what it finds and records the quantity found here.
  3. Anything not found: Head Office or the ROM checks the item's movements since the last stock take and records the cause.
  4. Close it once the stock has been adjusted. Items listed again and again show up under “Times listed”.
